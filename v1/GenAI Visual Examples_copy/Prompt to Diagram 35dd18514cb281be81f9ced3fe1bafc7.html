Prompt

Must-Have Elements in a Community Park

  • Inclusive Playground
    • Equipment for all abilities: swings, slides, climbing, sensory features.
  • Shaded Seating Areas
    • Benches, picnic tables, or pavilions with shade for rest and socializing.
  • Walking/Biking Paths
    • Looped paths for exercise, stroller access, and bikes — ideally with lighting.
  • Nature Play Zone
    • Logs, rocks, sand, and loose parts to encourage imaginative, sensory play.
  • Community Gathering Space
    • Open lawn or amphitheater for events, picnics, and performances.
  • Native Plant Gardens or Pollinator Habitat
    • Educational, low-maintenance landscaping with ecological benefits.
  • Water Play or Splash Area
    • A fun, interactive space for kids in summer — ideally with recirculated water.
  • Fitness/Exercise Station
    • Outdoor gym or multi-use zone for teens, adults, and seniors.
  • Restrooms & Drinking Water Fountain
    • Essential for comfort, accessibility, and long visits.
  • Lighting & Safety Features
    • Path lights, open sight lines, and clear signage for security and usability.
