Step 2: Access Google AI Studio

Google AI Studio is a pay-as-you-go platform (not monthly subscription) that gives you access to:

  • Nano Banana (image generation for Assignment 2)
  • Gemini AI models
  • App building tools (e.g. you can build Nana banana powered app without coding)

The interface is different from gemini.google.com. You need to go to: https://aistudio.google.com/

Cost reference:

  • Standard Nano Banana: ~$0.02 per image
  • Pro quality (4K): ~$0.12–$0.24 per image
  • No credit card required
  • No automatic charges
  • Service stops when credit runs out (won't charge you)
  • You can add additional money as needed in the future

Step 3: Navigate to Image Generation

  1. Click "Playground" in the left sidebar
  1. Select "Image Generation"

  1. Choose "Nano Banana 2" from the model dropdown (latest version, released last week)
  1. Click "Link API Key"

When you click "Link API Key", Google may ask you to add a credit card. This is for identity verification only — you will not be charged as long as you have credits remaining.

5. Verify your Credits in the Google Cloud Console

The credit doesn't show up inside the AI Studio interface; it lives in the Google Cloud Billing Console.

  • Go to the Google Cloud Billing Overview.
  • On the left sidebar, click Credits.
  • You should see a chart or list showing "Education Grant" or a similar name with your $50 balance.
  • Note: If you don't see it, check the "Billing Account" dropdown at the top of the page. The coupon often creates a newbilling account (e.g., named after your course or "Google Cloud Education") rather than adding to your default one.

Why did you need a Credit Card?

Google requires a valid payment method (credit card) to verify your identity and prevent bot abuse, even when you have a 100% discount via a grant.

  • Safety: You will not be charged as long as you have credits remaining.
  • Alerts: I recommend setting a "Budget Alert" in that same Billing Console to notify you if your usage ever exceeds the $50.
