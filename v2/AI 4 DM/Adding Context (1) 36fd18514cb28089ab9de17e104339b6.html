Adding Context (1)

Adding real-world context as the final step in AI image generation allows the core form and design of a building or space to remain consistent while enhancing the image with realistic surroundings, lighting, materials, and human activity. Separating context generation from the design process minimizes unwanted distortion of the architecture and results in more controllable, accurate, and applicable visualizations.

Examples

04: Atmosphere (Weather, Time of Day, Season)

Starting Office Building Render

Season Change

Transform the render into a winter setting while preserving the exact architecture, composition, and camera angle of the original image. Add a light layer of realistic snow accumulation on the rooftops, surrounding landscape, sidewalks, and roads while keeping the roads partially visible and functional. The trees should appear winter-like with sparse foliage or light snow coverage on branches. Adjust the lighting and atmosphere to create a cold but inviting seasonal mood with soft winter daylight and realistic reflections, shadows, and snow textures to maintain a photorealistic quality.

Time of Day

Transform the render into a nighttime scene while preserving the exact architecture, composition, and surrounding context of the original image. Illuminate the interior of the building with a soft warm glow that contrasts against the darker exterior environment. Through the windows, show subtle silhouettes of people working, moving, and occupying the space to create a sense of life and activity without overcrowding the scene. Enhance reflections, interior lighting gradients, and atmospheric shadows to create a cozy, inviting, and photorealistic nighttime architectural render.

Weather

Transform the render into a rainy, gloomy weather condition while preserving the exact architecture, composition, and overall scene layout. Add overcast skies, soft foggy atmosphere, wet pavement, puddles, and realistic rain effects to create a moody environment. Enhance reflections on roads, sidewalks, and glass surfaces from the wet conditions while slightly muting the overall colour palette to emphasize the gloomy atmosphere. The building should remain visually prominent and realistic within the scene, with subtle interior lighting and atmospheric depth maintaining a photorealistic architectural render quality.
