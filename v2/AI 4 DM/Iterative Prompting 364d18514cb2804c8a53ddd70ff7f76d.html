Iterative Prompting

Manual writing alone is often insufficient for effective AI prompt generation because it may convey specificity in an incomplete or inconsistent way. Precision is especially critical at this stage, since AI systems tend to “fill in gaps” when information is ambiguous or undefined. As a result, outputs may include unintended design elements or spatial qualities that were not part of the original intent.

This gap-filling behavior can be valuable during iterative exploration, as it may surface contextual responses or design opportunities the designer had not previously considered. However, when AI is used to refine a developed concept, unintended outputs can become counterproductive, making it harder to steer the model back toward the intended design direction.

Key Ideas:

  • 1-2 Tasks per prompt
  • Provide references
  • State constraints (What NOT to change VS. What the Change)
  • State specific & explicit changes
  • Separate geometric iteration from material iteration

Recommended Stages of Iteration:

[Geometry] → [Material] → [Furnishing/Details] → [Atmosphere] → [Rendered Views]

Example

Base Image

Prompt 1 [Geometry]

Modify only the roof form of the library to follow the curved sloping profile shown in the attached sketch, with the roof gradually rising upward toward the right side of the building. Preserve the exact materiality, glass facade treatment, lighting atmosphere, camera composition, and overall architectural style of the original image. Do not alter any other elements — only adjust the building form to incorporate the new curved upward-sloping roof geometry.

Prompt 2 [Material]

Change the primary structural and metal facade components of the building into a rich red brick material while preserving the existing architectural form and detailing. The sloped roof should remain metal, and any exposed structural elements or components that must remain metal should be changed into a dark matte black metal finish. Preserve everything else exactly as shown in the original image, including the building form, glass facade, lighting, atmosphere, surrounding context, camera angle, and overall composition.

Prompt 3 [Material 2]

Change the wood slats into a dark walnut.

Prompt 4 [Details/Facade]

Add a secondary facade layer onto the circled portions of the building using a black perforated metal cladding system that matches the existing dark black metal framing. The perforation pattern and density should be inspired by the facade treatment of Route 9 Library and Innovation Center by Perkins&Will, creating a refined layered screen effect with depth and filtered transparency. Preserve the exact building form, materials outside the specified areas, lighting, atmosphere, context, and camera composition.

Prompt 5 [Atmosphere]

Change the render to a night-time scene while preserving the exact building form, materials, context, and camera composition. Illuminate the interior with a soft warm ambient glow visible through the glass facade, creating strong contrast against the dark exterior. Emphasize the layered depth and perforation pattern of the black metal secondary facade by allowing warm interior light to subtly filter through and highlight the perforated cladding system. Use cinematic night lighting with soft reflections and atmospheric shadows to enhance the facade texture and overall architectural mood.

Prompt 6 [Alternate Rendered Views]

Generate four additional rendered views of the same library project that remain fully consistent with the original input image in terms of architecture, proportions, materials, lighting, structural logic, and overall design language. The new perspectives should feel like realistic viewpoints of the exact same building and must accurately reflect the established geometry and facade conditions from the reference render. Do not redesign or reinterpret the architecture.
Create: (1) an interior perspective looking outward through the perforated black metal facade with warm light filtering through the screen, (2) an interior render of the central staircase and atrium space emphasizing vertical circulation and openness, (3) a view from the top-right balcony overlooking the surrounding campus and facade, and (4) a welcoming entrance perspective highlighting the glass facade, brick structure, and perforated metal cladding at night. Maintain the same cinematic architectural visualization style, warm atmospheric lighting, and material consistency throughout all views.

Prompt 7 [Refinement]

Close the perforation in the circled area. There should not be a void in that space as the perforated metal facade covers the entire wall consistently. Do not change anything else in the image.
