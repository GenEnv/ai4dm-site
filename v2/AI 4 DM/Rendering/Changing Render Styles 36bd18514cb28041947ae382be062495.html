Changing Render Styles

Photorealistic to Photoshop Collage Render

Prompt

Transform the input photorealistic render into an artistic Photoshop collage-style architectural visualization inspired by the attached reference image. The final image should feel handcrafted and atmospheric rather than digitally perfect. Use layered textures, cutout materials, paper-like overlays, painterly shadows, grain, sketch-like edges, and mixed-media elements to create a collage aesthetic. Incorporate expressive vegetation, textured skies, and slightly abstract human figures similar to architectural presentation collages. Maintain the exact composition, perspective, geometry, and overall architectural form of the original render. Do not change the building design or layout — only reinterpret the image through a warm, serene, mixed-media collage style with strong texture variation and an artistic presentation quality.

Monochrome Pen/Linework Style Render

Prompt

Transform the input photorealistic render into a refined architectural CAD-style pen and linework illustration inspired by the attached Morris+Company reference render. The overall drawing style should feel clean, precise, and hand-drafted, resembling a high-quality architectural CAD block or conceptual line drawing rather than a heavily sketched illustration. Use light, elegant, and controlled linework with minimal visual heaviness. Avoid thick outlines, dense shadows, or overly dark sketch effects.
The primary theme colour should be deep red, with the building and architectural elements rendered predominantly in fine red linework on a pure white background. Preserve the exact composition, perspective, geometry, and architectural design from the original render. Texture and materiality should be communicated subtly through delicate line-based techniques such as fine hatching, stippling, crosshatching, and patterned fills while remaining consistent with the clean CAD-like aesthetic.
The surrounding context, vegetation, people, and ground plane should remain simplified and graphic, using lighter black and grey linework to create contrast with the red architecture. Depth and hierarchy should be achieved through variation in line density, spacing, and opacity rather than heavy shading. The final image should feel atmospheric, minimal, refined, and presentation-quality — combining the clarity of CAD drawings with the artistry of architectural linework illustration.

Linework Render to Illustration Style

Prompt

Transform the input isometric linework drawing into a stylized architectural illustration inspired by the attached reference image. Maintain the exact isometric perspective, geometry, composition, and architectural layout from the original drawing while converting the presentation style into a clean graphic illustration. The process should function similarly to colouring within the existing linework rather than redesigning or reinterpreting the drawing. Do not add, remove, or alter any architectural elements, furniture, objects, decorations, vegetation, or contextual components. Preserve all original linework exactly as provided. Convert the existing linework to crisp white lines and apply solid flat colour fills beneath the linework using the attached colour palette. Use minimal gradients or shading so the illustration remains graphic, clean, and diagrammatic. The people within the scene do not need to strictly follow the attached colour palette and can use more natural or varied colours to help the image feel lively while remaining visually cohesive. Avoid photorealism, textures, painterly effects, sketchiness, or additional detailing. The final output should feel like a polished architectural editorial illustration with strong clarity, controlled colour blocking, and the appearance of a carefully coloured architectural drawing.
